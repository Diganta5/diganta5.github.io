01 / ABOUT

A RESEARCHER'S NOTEBOOK

Somewhere between
theory and discovery.

Hello, I'm Diganta Samanta — a PhD research scholar in Physics at Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur.

My interests lie in statistical physics and quantum many-body theory. I am fascinated by emergent phenomena: how simple microscopic rules can give rise to fractionalized excitations, topological phases and unexpected collective behaviour.

This website is my corner of the internet to share research directions, computational projects, publications and notes from the journey.
